How to use the recommendation

Historical score: summarizes precipitation, snowfall, and wind on the latest date in the selected year.

Dispatch caution: this file contains historical observations only. Use a current forecast and road advisories before deciding whether to release LTL or consolidate a full truck.

Crop selection changes the risk tolerance used by this simple screening rule; it does not change the weather forecast. Confirm carrier capacity, road restrictions, local advisories, loading-site conditions, and customer urgency before dispatch.

Data source and storage

Source: Historical Open-Meteo CSV hosted in your GitHub repository, for St. Cloud, Minnesota (45.58875° N, 94.10379° W; elevation 319 m).

Fields present: hourly temperature, snowfall, wind speed at 10 m and 100 m, precipitation amount, and WMO weather code. Not present: precipitation probability and visibility; the dashboard marks these unavailable rather than estimating them.

Where stored: the CSV remains in GitHub. When loaded, parsed rows are held in browser memory only. For offline use, download the CSV and select it with “Load offline CSV.” The dashboard does not save data to a server, database, cookie, or local storage.

The year selector is populated from the years actually present in the CSV. The crop selector adjusts a simple historical screening score; it does not change observed weather.
