Source and limitations

Source: USDA National Agricultural Statistics Service (NASS), Quick Stats. The local CSV contains Minnesota state-level annual records for “CORN, GRAIN - PRODUCTION, MEASURED IN BU,” 2015–2025.

Original USDA query: USDA NASS Quick Stats results. General database: Quick Stats.

Local copy downloaded from the team's shared project CSV on November 19, 2026. The shared file was added to the team repository on November 17, 2026; the original USDA extraction date was not recorded in that file.

This is a statewide historical production signal, not a forecast or a measure of available inventory, local supplier capacity, corn prices, or BAIT's actual ingredient demand. Higher production does not automatically mean lower prices or guaranteed supply. Some NASS county-level data may be withheld to protect confidentiality.
